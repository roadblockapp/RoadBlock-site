About RoadBlock

Make focus the easy choice.

RoadBlock is a rewards-based distracted-driving prevention concept. Our branded phone mount uses an NFC tag to help drivers open a session before a trip, then rewards consistent participation.

✓ Positive motivation, not phone lockouts
✓ Designed to complement compatible CarPlay features
✓ Progress and rewards in one dashboard
Four simple steps

How it works

Designed to make focused driving a repeatable habit.

01

Mount

Place your phone securely in the RoadBlock mount before setting off.

02

Tap

Tap the NFC notification to open RoadBlock and start a session.

03

Focus

Keep attention on the road. Interact with your phone only when safely parked.

04

Earn

Eligible session time helps build points, levels, and reward progress.
